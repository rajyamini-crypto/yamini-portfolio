02 Case Studies

Three requirements-to-adoption stories, told the way a BA documents them: trigger, approach, artifacts, outcome.

CASE 01 — ACCESS & CONTENT GOVERNANCE
Secure Digital Asset Management Framework
Meta Platforms · Project Analyst

Trigger

Confidential digital content needed a governed access and distribution model.

Approach

Led stakeholder requirements gathering to engineer a secure DAM framework, defining an Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) model and automated watermarking rules.

Outcome ABAC and automated watermarking implemented to reduce unauthorized distribution of confidential content.

Mobile Banking, Cross-Border Wire & SIFI Reporting
Comerica Bank · Business Analyst
